# Code of conduct

We want everyone to feel welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity.

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, sexual language or imagery, or personal attacks.
- Don't share other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem, email support@bifrostorbit.com. We'll read every report and keep it confidential.
