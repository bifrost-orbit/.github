# Getting help

- **Documentation:** [docs.bifrostorbit.com](https://docs.bifrostorbit.com) has guides for every SDK and starter.
- **Questions:** ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/bifrost-orbit/.github/discussions).
- **Bugs and ideas:** open an issue in the repository it's about.
- **Your account or your data:** email support@bifrostorbit.com.
